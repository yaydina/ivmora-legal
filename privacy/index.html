This policy explains what data the Ivmora app (“the app”, “we”) handles, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to it.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: if you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address and (Google only) profile photo to create your account.
  • Habit data: the habits you create, check-in logs, notes, daily reflections, streaks, settings, and any family member profiles you add. This is stored on your device and, when you are signed in, synced to our cloud database (Google Firebase / Firestore).
  • Purchase data: subscription status, handled by Google Play Billing / the App Store and RevenueCat. We never see your payment card details.
  • Reminders: reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; they do not send data to us.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no advertising SDKs.

2. How we use it

Solely to run the app: showing your progress, calculating streaks and momentum, generating coaching messages, syncing across your devices, and managing your Premium subscription. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to operate the app: Google (Firebase sign-in and cloud storage), Apple (Sign in with Apple), and RevenueCat (subscription management). They process data only to provide those services. We may disclose data if required by law.

4. Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you delete the app or its data. Cloud data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account) or by following the instructions at https://yaydina.github.io/ivmora-legal/delete-account/. Deleted data is removed from active systems immediately and from backups within 30 days. Purchase records may be retained by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required for billing and legal purposes.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. The Family feature lets an adult keep habit lists for family members locally on their own account; those members do not have logins and we do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children.

6. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Local data is kept in your device’s app-sandboxed storage.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data from within the app, or contact us at support@stmatrix.com.tr. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK or Türkiye under KVKK), you may have additional rights such as objection or restriction of processing and the right to complain to your data protection authority.

8. Changes

If we make material changes we will update this page and notify you in the app before they take effect.
