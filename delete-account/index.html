Delete your Ivmora account

App: Ivmora – Daily Habit Coach (previously listed as “Accustom – Daily Habit Coach”) · Developer: STMatrix

You can delete your Ivmora account and the data linked to it at any time, either inside the app or by contacting us.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app

  1. Open Ivmora and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm and tap the delete button.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, email support@stmatrix.com.tr from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject “Ivmora account deletion”. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Google or Apple sign-in identifier, name and email address).
  • Your habits, check-in history, notes, daily reflections, family member profiles and settings stored in the cloud.
  • The same data stored on your device is erased when you delete the account in the app.

What may be kept

  • Purchase records held by Apple or Google and by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) as required for billing, tax and fraud-prevention. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in the App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Questions? Contact support@stmatrix.com.tr · stmatrix.com.tr
